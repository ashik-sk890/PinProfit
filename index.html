How long until I see clicks or my first sale?+
Pinterest is a search engine, not an instant viral lottery. Most consistent sellers start seeing initial impressions and early clicks in Weeks 1–2 as pins get indexed, with traffic compounding across Weeks 3–8 as boards build topical relevance. Individual results always depend on your niche, product quality, and consistency.
Should I sell my digital products on Etsy or Gumroad?+
Module 01 walks through both options clearly. Gumroad is completely free to start with zero listing fees and simple link sharing; Etsy gives you built-in marketplace shoppers plus a $0.20 per-listing fee. Many creators start on Gumroad and expand to Etsy (or use both together).

Yes. Static vertical 2:3 graphic pins with clean mockups and strong typography still drive massive outbound clicks on Pinterest without ever stepping in front of a camera.
